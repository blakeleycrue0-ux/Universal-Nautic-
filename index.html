Universal Nautic

Bespoke craftsmanship for exceptional yachts.

Based in Palma de Mallorca, Universal Nautic specialises in bespoke interior upholstery, deck fit-out and carpets & flooring for superyachts. We work directly with owners, captains, interior designers and shipyards, sourcing the finest leathers, textiles and carpets from across Europe and managing every stage of a refit with the same attention as a new build.
