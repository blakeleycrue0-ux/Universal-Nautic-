I The house

For more than twenty-five years, Universal Nautic has furnished superyachts from its own workshop in Mallorca — one seam, one cushion, one saloon at a time.

We are upholsterers, machinists, cover makers and carpet fitters working under one roof in Son Bugadelles, Santa Ponsa. Interior and exterior, leather and canvas, a single cushion or a complete refit: every piece is made by the people who will answer for it.

Our approach has not changed since the beginning of 2000 — the best possible service and product, delivered with a personal, straightforward manner by craftspeople who still work at the bench.

The stitch — from material to finished piece

  1. 01 — Material

    It begins with the hide: full grain, chosen by hand, its surface read under a raking light.

  2. 02 — Craft

    The needle draws the seam one stitch at a time, the thread set into the leather rather than laid on it.

  3. 03 — Line

    Stitch length, tension and distance from the edge held constant — around every corner, on every cushion aboard.

  4. 04 — Form

    A line of thread becomes a piped, crowned seat cushion, made to sit exactly where it was measured.

  5. Excellence lives
    in the details.

IV Interior craftsmanship

Precision you can sit on. Comfort you can measure.

From a single re-covered armchair to the complete furnishing of an interior, we work from the frame outwards: foam, form, fabric, seam.

  1. 01

    Upholstery

    Seating, headboards, panels and decorative cushions, in leather and textile.

V Exterior living

Made for salt, sun and the long Mediterranean afternoon.

Exterior work is judged in the harshest light there is. Cushions must drain and dry; covers must sit taut in a breeze; seams must survive a season of sun and salt — and still look tailored at the end of it.

  • Seating and sunbathing cushions
  • Custom awnings and sunshades
  • Stainless steel biminis and structures
  • Dodgers and spray hoods
  • Tender covers
  • Screen and privacy covers
  • Soft-lined covers for delicate surfaces
  • Teak furniture, shown in our Garpa showroom

VI Project execution

One point of contact. Every trade coordinated.

For a full interior refit we take responsibility for the whole furnishing package — coordinating every trade involved, and working with items supplied by the owner where required.

  1. 01

    Advice

    Material selection in our showroom, with samples from leading textile and leather houses.

  2. 02

    Design

    Visualisation of bespoke pieces before a single panel is cut.

  3. 03

    Workshop

    Fabrication in our own 1,000 m² workshop in Son Bugadelles.

  4. 04

    Aboard

    Fitting by our own team: upholsterers, cover makers and carpet fitters.

  5. 05

    Coordination

    All trades managed as a single project, owner-supplied items included.
