Who we are

Universal Nautic is a Mallorca-based specialist in bespoke interior upholstery, deck fit-out and carpets & flooring for superyachts. We work directly with owners, captains, interior designers and shipyards on new builds and refits, sourcing fine leathers, textiles and carpets from across Europe.

As the official Sylka distributor in Spain, we have direct access to bespoke carpet and flooring solutions built for the marine environment, alongside strong relationships with suppliers across Europe that let us source and deliver quickly, including for emergency repairs and replacement work while a yacht is in service.

Craft

Every panel, cushion and carpet is cut, stitched and finished by hand in our own workshop before it goes on board.

Precision

Marine interiors leave no margin for error. Templates, tolerances and fittings are treated with the same rigour as the vessel itself.

Materials

Fine leathers, designer textiles and hand-tufted carpets, sourced from established European makers and mills.

Experience

Years of refit and new-build work across the yachting industry, from single cabins to full interior projects.

Mediterranean

Based in Palma de Mallorca, at the centre of the Mediterranean refit season, with reach across Europe.
