Approach

We manage full interior refits and new-build projects end to end — covering all trades and owner-supplied items — so that cabins, saloons and crew areas are finished to a single, consistent standard. Fabric, leather and carpet are sourced from established European makers and cut and stitched by hand in our own workshop before installation.

Thanks to strong relationships with suppliers across Europe, any fabric or carpet can be sourced quickly for emergency repairs and replacement areas while a yacht is in service.
