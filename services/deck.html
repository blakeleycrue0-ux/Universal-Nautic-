Deck

Exterior-grade cushions, teak furniture and carpentry, awnings and sunshades, built to withstand the Mediterranean sun without compromising on line or finish.

Approach

Deck furniture and shading are exposed to sun, salt and constant use, so materials and construction are chosen accordingly. We build cushions, teak elements, awnings and sunshades to marine-grade specification, matched to the yacht's exterior design and finished by hand.

As with our interior work, fabrics and materials can be sourced quickly through our supplier network for repairs or seasonal refreshes.
