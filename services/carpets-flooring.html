Approach

As official Sylka distributor in Spain, we supply and fit carpets and flooring engineered for the marine environment, alongside hand-tufted carpets and rugs for interior spaces. Every installation is measured, cut and fitted on board by our own team.

Our supplier relationships allow us to source matching carpet and flooring quickly for emergency repairs or replacement areas, minimising disruption while a yacht is in service.
